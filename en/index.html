Enrico Schulz

Bridging sales, project management & technology

UNDERSTAND.TRANSLATE.IMPLEMENT.

I don’t fit into a single box – and that’s no coincidence. For more than twelve years, I managed sites and a region in professional vehicle reconditioning, led partner companies with 115 employees operationally and digitised workflows. I then deliberately trained in project management, commerce and web development, and most recently brought customers, sales and technology together on site at Enpal.

My approach is an open system: clear structures and proven methods as the foundation – and the openness to take on new ideas, question processes and improve them continuously. This is how I understand what customers, teams and technology need, translate between them – and implement what really works in the end.
